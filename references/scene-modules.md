# Scene detail cues

Use only cues present in the crop.

- **People:** anatomy, skin planes, hair groups, garment panels, seams, weave, folds, shoes, and contact shadows.
- **Architecture:** structural line families, facade modules, joints, glazing depth, interior variation, reflection orientation, and atmospheric falloff.
- **Stage/interior:** fixture housings, beam origin, floor reflections, textile construction, screen boundaries, cables, stands, and depth separation.
- **Nature:** support hierarchy, species-scale variation, leaf clusters, bark or stem construction, ground contact, and haze.
- **Product/vehicle:** silhouette, panel gaps, fasteners, controls, machining direction, material transitions, and source-supported wear.
- **Art/print:** original mark-making, pigment or ink behavior, paper or canvas only where visible, and style-consistent edge language.

Attach every requested detail to a visible subject, support, surface, light, or depth plane. Omit the module when that relationship is absent.
