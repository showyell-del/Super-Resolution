# Scene cues for the subject and material layers

Use only cues visible in the crop and only at a scale the viewer can resolve.

- **People:** face planes, eyes and mouth appropriate to expression; hair groups, fingers, joints, garment panels, seams, folds, body contact and cast shadow. Photographic skin has restrained local variation; stylized skin follows its art medium.
- **Architecture:** facade bay cadence, shared vanishing directions, real joints, glazing depth, interior variation, surface orientation, environmental reflection, distance haze.
- **Stage/interior:** fixture and beam origin, LED screen boundary, performer overlap, fabric construction, floor reflection, stands/cables only where visible.
- **Nature:** trunk-to-branch-to-leaf support, irregular species-scale clusters, bark/stem construction, ground contact, depth and motion.
- **Product/vehicle:** silhouette, panel gaps, controls, fasteners, machining direction, roughness transitions, source-supported wear.
- **Art/print:** original line quality, stroke scale, pigment or ink behavior, paper/canvas texture only if evidenced, intentional simplification.

If a region is too small, out of focus, moving, or hazy, preserve those optics. Fine detail should taper with projected size and illumination; it should not become equally crisp across the frame.
